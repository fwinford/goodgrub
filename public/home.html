How It Works

For Requesters

  1. Fill out the request form with vendor, food, time window, and email
  2. CommonPlate attempts to send a confirmation email; delivery is not guaranteed
  3. Another student places your order on GrubHub using their meal swipes
  4. When someone helps, CommonPlate attempts to email the pickup details; delivery is not guaranteed
  5. Pick up your food!

For Givers

  1. Browse active meal requests on the homepage
  2. Click a request to see full details
  3. Place the order using your meal swipes on GrubHub
  4. Enter the order number and estimated pickup time
  5. CommonPlate attempts to email the requester the order details
